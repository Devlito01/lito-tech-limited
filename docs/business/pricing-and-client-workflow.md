## Simple Client Reply Workflow

When a client contacts you:

1. thank them
2. ask what they need
3. ask about timeline or scope
4. recommend the best service
5. send a quote or proposal

## First Reply Template

```text
Hello, thank you for reaching out to Lito Tech Limited.

We’d be glad to help. Please share a little about what you need, and we’ll guide you from there.
```

## Pricing Reply Template

```text
Hello, thank you for reaching out to Lito Tech Limited.

Pricing depends on the type of project, the number of pages or features needed, and the overall scope of work. Once we understand what you need, we can recommend the best option and provide a clear quote.

Our projects typically start from:
- Landing pages: from $100
- Business websites: from $150
- WordPress websites: from $250
- Web applications: from $500+

If you’d like, you can share what you need and we’ll guide you on the best next step.
```

## Simple Proposal Message

```text
Hello,

Thank you for your interest in working with Lito Tech Limited.

Based on your request, we would recommend a [service type] package for this project. This would be the best fit for your goals and the type of outcome you want to achieve.

Proposed starting price: [$ amount]

This would cover:
- design and structure
- development/setup
- content placement
- revisions based on the agreed scope

If you are happy with this direction, the next step would be to confirm the project details so we can move forward.

Best regards,
Lito Tech Limited
```

## Recommended Client Workflow

1. inquiry comes in
2. you reply
3. client explains the project
4. you recommend the right package
5. you send a quote or proposal
6. client accepts
7. you collect payment/start fee
8. project begins

## Key Reminder

Do not rush to price too low just to accept every project.

It is better to have:

- a clear minimum price
- a professional offer
- a confident process

That will help you attract better clients over time.
