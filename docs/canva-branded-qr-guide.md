# Canva Branded QR Guide

This guide shows how to create a branded QR code in Canva for your own website or for a client.

## What You Need

Before you start, have these ready:

- the link you want the QR code to open
- the brand name
- optional brand colors
- optional logo

## Step 1: Open Canva

1. Go to https://www.canva.com/
2. Log in
3. Click `Create a design`

Choose the design size you want:

- `Instagram Post` for square QR cards
- `Business Card` for print
- `Custom Size` if needed

## Step 2: Set the Background

1. Click the page background
2. Choose a clean background color

Good options:

- white
- light gray
- very light blue

## Step 3: Add a Main Shape

1. Go to `Elements`
2. Search for `rounded rectangle`
3. Add one to the page
4. Resize it to fit your layout

Use it as:

- a dark left panel
- a top banner
- or a white card section

Good premium colors:

- navy
- deep blue
- dark charcoal

## Step 4: Add the QR Code

You can do this in two ways.

### Option A: Use Canva QR Code App

1. In Canva, open `Apps`
2. Search for `QR Code`
3. Open it
4. Paste your website link
5. Click `Generate code`

### Option B: Upload Your Own QR Code

1. Generate the plain QR code from a site like:
   - https://goqr.me/
2. Download it as PNG
3. In Canva, go to `Uploads`
4. Upload the QR image
5. Place it in the design

## Step 5: Position the QR Code

Place the QR code in a clean area with enough space around it.

Best layout ideas:

- center for square design
- right side for horizontal design

Important:

- do not stretch the QR unevenly
- keep it large enough to scan easily
- leave white space around it

## Step 6: Add the Text

Keep the wording short.

Simple text options:

- `SCAN TO VISIT`
- `Lito Tech Limited`
- `Scan with your phone camera`

Premium wording option:

- `SCAN TO VISIT`
- `Lito Tech Limited`
- `Discover our work and start your next project with us.`

## Step 7: Style the Text

Use:

- small text for labels
- bigger bold text for the brand name
- simple readable text for support lines

Good style choices:

- all caps for `SCAN TO VISIT`
- bold for brand name
- lighter text color for supporting text

## Step 8: Align Everything

1. Select the elements
2. Use Canva `Position`
3. Align them cleanly

Check spacing between:

- title
- brand name
- QR code
- support text

Keep the layout clean and balanced.

## Step 9: Download the Final Design

1. Click `Share`
2. Click `Download`
3. Choose `PNG`

Use:

- `PNG` for WhatsApp, email, social media, and client delivery
- `PDF Print` if a printer asks for it

## Step 10: Test the QR Code

Before sharing it:

1. open the exported image
2. scan it with your phone
3. confirm it opens the correct link
4. make sure the image is clear and readable

Never send it without testing.

## Best Layout Types

### Square Layout

Best for:

- WhatsApp
- social media
- flyers
- simple sharing

Suggested structure:

- top text
- QR code in center
- small support text at bottom

### Horizontal Layout

Best for:

- business cards
- banners
- print materials

Suggested structure:

- text on left
- QR code on right

## Best Practices

- keep the QR code black on white if possible
- do not add too much text
- do not make the QR too small
- keep branding simple
- test scan before sharing

4. insert the QR code
5. add short branded text
6. align everything neatly
